# Bangla 1 · সন্ধি

### The past questions

| Paper                        | Question                         | Answer       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------ |
| BUET DMTCL, 19.06.2026 (Q5)  | "পরীক্ষা" শব্দের সন্ধি বিচ্ছেদ   | পরি + ঈক্ষা  |
| BUET DMTCL, 19.06.2026 (Q6)  | "অধিকার" শব্দের সন্ধি বিচ্ছেদ    | অধি + কার    |
| BUET DMTCL, 19.06.2026 (Q20) | "সূর্যোদয়" শব্দের সন্ধি বিচ্ছেদ | সূর্য + উদয় |
| BAEC Engineer, 25.09.2026    | সন্ধি বিচ্ছেদ (one question)     | —            |

### Picture it

Think of two train carriages being joined. The last sound of the first word and the first sound of the second word are the two hooks. When they lock, the hooks change shape: two "অ/আ" hooks melt into one "আ", an "ই" hook bends into a "য-ফলা". **সন্ধি is only about the meeting point.** Look at the joint, never the whole word.

Three kinds of joints: vowel + vowel (স্বরসন্ধি), consonant on either side (ব্যঞ্জনসন্ধি), and a visarga (ঃ) on the left (বিসর্গসন্ধি). Some joints break the rules and are simply remembered: **নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ**.

### Understand it step by step

1. **Find the joint.** Split the word where two meanings meet: সূর্য | উদয়.
2. **Name the two sounds** at the joint: অ (end of সূর্য) + উ (start of উদয়).
3. **Apply the rule** from the table. অ + উ = ও → সূর্যোদয়.
4. **Check in reverse:** the split must give two real words or roots.

**স্বরসন্ধি (vowel + vowel)**

| Meeting           | Result | Example                                         |
| ----------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| অ/আ + অ/আ         | আ      | হিম + আলয় = হিমালয়; বিদ্যা + আলয় = বিদ্যালয় |
| ই/ঈ + ই/ঈ         | ঈ      | রবি + ইন্দ্র = রবীন্দ্র; পরি + ঈক্ষা = পরীক্ষা  |
| অ/আ + ই/ঈ         | এ      | নর + ইন্দ্র = নরেন্দ্র; মহা + ঈশ = মহেশ         |
| অ/আ + উ/ঊ         | ও      | সূর্য + উদয় = সূর্যোদয়; যথা + উচিত = যথোচিত   |
| অ/আ + এ/ঐ         | ঐ      | জন + এক = জনৈক                                  |
| অ/আ + ও/ঔ         | ঔ      | বন + ঔষধি = বনৌষধি                              |
| ই/ঈ + other vowel | য-ফলা  | অতি + অন্ত = অত্যন্ত; ইতি + আদি = ইত্যাদি       |
| উ/ঊ + other vowel | ব-ফলা  | সু + আগত = স্বাগত; অনু + এষণ = অন্বেষণ          |
| এ + অ             | অয়    | নে + অন = নয়ন                                  |
| ঐ + অ             | আয়    | নৈ + অক = নায়ক                                 |
| ও + অ             | অব     | পো + অন = পবন                                   |
| ঔ + অ             | আব     | পৌ + অক = পাবক                                  |

**ব্যঞ্জনসন্ধি (a consonant at the joint)**

| Example                              | What happened                     |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| দিক্ + অন্ত = দিগন্ত                 | ক্ → গ্ before a vowel            |
| বাক্ + দান = বাগ্দান                 | ক্ → গ্ before a voiced consonant |
| উৎ + জ্বল = উজ্জ্বল; সৎ + জন = সজ্জন | ৎ → জ্ before জ                   |
| উৎ + নতি = উন্নতি                    | ৎ → ন্ before ন                   |
| সম্ + চয় = সঞ্চয়                   | ম্ → ঞ্ before চ-বর্গ             |

**বিসর্গসন্ধি (ঃ on the left)**

| Example             | What happened                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| মনঃ + যোগ = মনোযোগ  | অঃ → ও before a voiced sound          |
| নিঃ + চয় = নিশ্চয় | ঃ → শ্ before চ                       |
| দুঃ + গতি = দুর্গতি | ইঃ/উঃ → র্ before a voiced sound      |
| নিঃ + রস = নীরস     | ঃ drops before র, the vowel lengthens |

### Bangla core

সন্ধি মানে পাশাপাশি দুটি ধ্বনির মিলন। শুধু মিলনের জায়গাটা দেখো: স্বর-স্বর হলে স্বরসন্ধি, ব্যঞ্জন থাকলে ব্যঞ্জনসন্ধি, বিসর্গ থাকলে বিসর্গসন্ধি।

### Mindmap

```mindmap
সন্ধি
  **Method**
    find the joint
    name the 2 sounds
    apply rule, check reverse
  **স্বরসন্ধি**
    অ/আ + অ/আ = আ (হিমালয়)
    অ/আ + ই = এ, + উ = ও
    ই + other = য-ফলা (অত্যন্ত)
    উ + other = ব-ফলা (স্বাগত)
    এ/ঐ/ও/ঔ + অ = অয়/আয়/অব/আব
  **ব্যঞ্জনসন্ধি**
    দিক্ + অন্ত = দিগন্ত
    উৎ + জ্বল = উজ্জ্বল
    সম্ + চয় = সঞ্চয়
  **বিসর্গসন্ধি**
    মনঃ + যোগ = মনোযোগ
    নিঃ + চয় = নিশ্চয়
    দুঃ + গতি = দুর্গতি
  **নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ**
    গবাক্ষ, অন্যান্য, পরস্পর
    বৃহস্পতি, ষোড়শ, মনীষা
```

### Remember it

- **"আ-এ-ও-ঐ-ঔ"**: অ/আ meets আ, ই, উ, এ, ও → আ, এ, ও, ঐ, ঔ.
- **"ই bends to য, উ bends to ব"**: অত্যন্ত, স্বাগত.
- **"এ-ঐ-ও-ঔ + অ = অয়-আয়-অব-আব"**: নয়ন, নায়ক, পবন, পাবক.
- **Exception list = "গো-অন্য-কুল-মার্ত, পর-বৃহৎ-ষট্-মনস্"**.

### Exam trap

- **পরীক্ষা** is পরি + ঈক্ষা, not পরী + ক্ষা. Split at a meaningful root.
- **গবাক্ষ** is নিপাতনে সিদ্ধ. Rule-based "গো + অক্ষ = গবক্ষ" would be wrong; the real word must be memorised.
- **স্বাগত** = সু + আগত, not স্ব + আগত.
- **নীরস** (ঃ drops, ই lengthens) vs **নিরস**: only নীরস is correct.

# English 1 · Parts of speech

### The past questions

| Paper                                        | Question type                                                                          | Count   |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| BUET DMTCL, 19.06.2026 (Q42, 43, 55, 58, 60) | Identify the part of speech of the underlined word: verb, adjective, noun, conjunction | 5 of 20 |
| BAEC Engineer, 25.09.2026                    | Identify the part of speech                                                            | 1–2     |

### Picture it

A word is an actor, and a part of speech is the **role** it plays in this scene. The same actor (the word "fast") can play a hero in one film and a villain in another. You can't name the role from the actor's face; you must watch the scene. **Read the sentence, then ask what job the word is doing.**

### Understand it step by step

1. **Find the job, not the dictionary label.** Ask: does it name (noun), replace a noun (pronoun), show action or state (verb), describe a noun (adjective), describe a verb/adjective/adverb (adverb), link a noun to the sentence (preposition), join clauses (conjunction), or show a sudden feeling (interjection)?
2. **Preposition vs conjunction:** a preposition takes a noun or pronoun after it; a conjunction takes a full clause (subject + verb).
   - "I have waited **since** Monday." → preposition (Monday is a noun).
   - "He left **since** he was tired." → conjunction (he was tired = clause).
3. **Adjective vs adverb:** an adjective describes a noun; an adverb describes a verb.
   - "a **fast** car" → adjective; "He runs **fast**" → adverb.
4. **Gerund vs participle (the "-ing" trap):**
   - Gerund = an "-ing" word used as a **noun**: "**Swimming** is good exercise."
   - Present participle = an "-ing" word used as an **adjective**: "a **sleeping** child" (the child is sleeping).
5. **"-ly" does not always mean adverb.** friendly, lovely, lonely, costly, silly, ugly, manly, likely are adjectives.

| Word  | Job 1                        | Job 2                        | Job 3                                             |
| ----- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| fast  | adjective: a fast train      | adverb: run fast             | verb: Muslims fast in Ramadan / noun: a long fast |
| but   | conjunction: poor but honest | preposition: all but him     | adverb: He is but a child (= only)                |
| since | preposition: since 2020      | conjunction: since you asked | adverb: I have not met him since                  |
| that  | pronoun: That is mine        | adjective: that book         | conjunction: I know that he came                  |
| round | noun: the first round        | preposition: round the table | verb: round the number                            |
| well  | adverb: She sings well       | adjective: I am well         | interjection: Well, let's go                      |

### Bangla core

একটি শব্দ কোন পদ, তা বাক্যে তার কাজ দেখে ঠিক হয়, শব্দ দেখে নয়। পরে noun থাকলে preposition, পুরো clause থাকলে conjunction।

### Mindmap

```mindmap
Parts of speech
  **Rule**
    job in sentence, not dictionary
    same word, many roles
  **Preposition vs conjunction**
    + noun → preposition
    + clause → conjunction
    since, before, after, but
  **Adjective vs adverb**
    describes noun → adjective
    describes verb → adverb
    fast, hard, early, late
  **-ing words**
    noun job → gerund
    adjective job → participle
  **-ly adjectives**
    friendly, lovely, lonely
    costly, silly, likely
  **Interjection**
    Alas! Hurrah! Well,
```

### Remember it

- **"Noun after → preposition; clause after → conjunction."**
- **"Ing as a thing = gerund; ing that describes = participle."**
- **"Friendly is a friend of nouns"**: most -ly words from nouns are adjectives.

### Exam trap

- Marking "friendly" as an adverb because of "-ly".
- Marking every "-ing" word as a verb. "Walking is healthy" → noun (gerund).
- "All **but** John came" → preposition, not conjunction (John is a noun, no clause).
- "He is **well**" (health) → adjective, not adverb.

### Part 4 · Practice set (10 MCQ, 6 min)

Name the part of speech of the word in bold.

1. He ran **fast** to catch the bus. (a) adjective (b) adverb (c) verb (d) noun
2. They will **fast** on Friday. (a) noun (b) adjective (c) verb (d) adverb
3. All **but** John came to the party. (a) conjunction (b) adverb (c) preposition (d) pronoun
4. She is a **friendly** girl. (a) adverb (b) adjective (c) noun (d) verb
5. **Walking** is good exercise. (a) verb (b) participle (c) noun (d) adjective
6. I saw a **sleeping** child. (a) noun (b) adjective (c) verb (d) adverb
7. I have not seen him **since** Monday. (a) conjunction (b) adverb (c) preposition (d) noun
8. He left early **since** he was tired. (a) preposition (b) conjunction (c) adverb (d) pronoun
9. **That** book is mine. (a) pronoun (b) conjunction (c) adjective (d) adverb
10. **Alas**! He is dead. (a) adverb (b) interjection (c) noun (d) conjunction

#### Solution

1. **(b)** describes the verb "ran".
2. **(c)** the action "to fast".
3. **(c)** followed by a noun (John), meaning "except".
4. **(b)** describes "girl"; -ly but an adjective.
5. **(c)** gerund, the subject of the sentence.
6. **(b)** participle describing "child".
7. **(c)** followed by a noun (Monday).
8. **(b)** followed by a clause (he was tired).
9. **(c)** describes "book" (demonstrative adjective).
10. **(b)** sudden feeling.

Score 1 each. Wrong = −0.25.
