# BUET Non-Departmental MCQ — DMTCL Section Engineer — 19.06.2026

Source: DIAGONAL Job Solution (shared 6 Oct 2026). Dhaka Mass Transit Company Ltd (DMTCL), Section Engineer, all engineering departments (EEE, ECE, CSE, Telecom, Data Comm & Networking, ME, Power, Civil, RAC). Venue and question setter: BUET. Non-departmental part: **80 MCQ × 1 = 80 marks**.

### Picture it

Think of this paper as a four-door entrance test before the real (departmental) exam. Three doors are the same size — Bangla, English, Math, 20 each. The fourth door is a mixed bag: GK, a little engineering, a little IQ. Every door opens with SSC-level keys. The paper is not hard; it punishes carelessness and slowness, not lack of knowledge.

### Understand it step by step — the math shortcuts used

1. **Q61:** x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² − 2 = 9 − 2 = 7.
2. **Q63:** (a − b)² = (a + b)² − 4ab = 16 − 4 = 12.
3. **Q66:** GP sum 2 + 4 + … = 2(2ⁿ − 1) = 254 → 2ⁿ = 128 → n = 7.
4. **Q72:** loss 25% → SP = 75% of CP → CP = 150 ÷ 0.75 = 200.
5. **Q73:** l = 2w, 2w² = 98 → w = 7, l = 14 → perimeter 2(21) = 42.
6. **Q74:** 92.4 km/h × (10/60) h = 15.4 km = 15,400 m.
7. **Q78:** tan θ = height/shadow = 6/(6√3) = 1/√3 → θ = 30°.
8. **Q79:** numbers 2k, 3k → LCM = 6k = 24 → k = 4 = HCF.

## What this means for you

- **Bank IT written papers (your main target)** test the non-IT part as essays, translation and GK — not MCQ grammar. This BUET paper is a different format.
- **BUET-set exams** (DMTCL, power-sector companies, other engineering-post recruitments) use this exact MCQ shape. If you apply to any BUET-set programmer/engineer post, this 80-mark block can decide the shortlist, because everyone scores well in the departmental part.
- Effort needed is small: one SSC Bangla grammar book, one English grammar book, SSC general math. The risk is speed and silly errors, not difficulty.

### Remember it

- **"20-20-20-20"** — Bangla, English, Math, rest.
- **Bangla = ব্যাকরণ only:** "সন্ধি-সমাস-কারক, পদ-উৎস-অর্থ."
- **Math = "Geo-Alge-Arith":** geometry first (7 of 20).
- The key is not the truth: Q68 and Q75 prove it.

### Exam trap

- Q75-type: check **every** option, not the first one that fits — two options can satisfy the condition.
- Q68-type: when no option matches your answer, re-read the question once, then pick the closest logical reading; don't change a correct calculation to match a wrong key.
- Q45: "The man / A man is mortal" — a general truth about mankind takes **no article**: "Man is mortal."
- Q47: with "everyone", the traditional answer is **his** (formal); "their" is modern usage but marked wrong in BD exams.
