# UX Audit — Tech Buddy Through a Job Seeker's Eyes

Date: Nov 2026. Method: a new candidate preparing for a government IT post walks through the web app on a phone
(Pixel 7 viewport, Playwright). The walk covers landing, circulars, a circular, registration, goal, dashboard,
study, daily exam, result, practice and profile. Screens were reviewed one by one, and every finding was
reproduced before it was fixed.

## What a job seeker needs, and how well the site delivers

| #   | Need                                                         | Before | After | Notes                                                                                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | :----: | :---: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Find circulars that fit me, fast                             |  3.5   |  4.5  | Filters took a full phone screen; nothing was personalised. Now compact, plus "Circulars for you".              |
| 2   | Understand a circular: deadline, posts, salary, how to apply |  3.5   |  4.5  | The Apply button and deadline date were about two screens down on a phone. Now above the fold.                  |
| 3   | Never miss a deadline                                        |   3    |   4   | Save, plus a dashboard alert 7 days before. New: an "Add to calendar" file with reminders a day and 2 h before. |
| 4   | Prepare: notes, daily exam, practice, past papers            |   4    |  4.5  | The exam timer started on the first tap. Now a rules screen comes first.                                        |
| 5   | See my progress and what to fix                              |   4    |  4.5  | New users saw five zeros and three empty charts. Now a 3-step "Get started" list.                               |

Overall: **about 3.0 / 5 before, 4.3 / 5 after.** The core loop of circulars → study → exam → review → weak topics
was already strong. The friction was in the first five minutes on a phone.

## Findings and fixes

| Severity | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Critical | **Every signed-in page was 1004 px wide on a 412 px phone.** The one-column grid's `aside` kept `min-width: auto`, so the scrolling nav strip widened the page. Pages scrolled sideways and dialogs opened off-screen; the "Ready to start?" dialog couldn't be reached. | `grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]` and `min-w-0` on the sidebar, in the app and admin layouts. A sweep of 18 pages now shows no overflow.                                                                                                  |
| High     | **The preparation goal did nothing.** Onboarding promised to "personalise your dashboard", but the goal was only shown as a label.                                                                                                                                       | `GET /dashboard/me` returns `recommendedCirculars`: open now, in the goal's categories, not already saved. The dashboard shows "Circulars for you", and onboarding now lands on the dashboard.                                      |
| High     | **New-user dashboard was a wall of zeros.** Five 0-value stats and three empty charts, with the "take today's exam" action below them.                                                                                                                                   | Today's exam card first (Start / Resume / score). Then a "Get started" checklist (first exam, first topic, first saved circular) that disappears when done. Charts appear only after the first exam. Stats use 2 columns on phones. |
| High     | **On phones, account pages and log out were hard to find.** The hamburger menu had public links only; the account strip hid most of its items off-screen, with labels wrapped onto two lines.                                                                            | The menu adds a "My account" section and Log out when signed in. Strip labels stay on one line.                                                                                                                                     |
| High     | **Circular page: the answers a candidate wants were buried.** Deadline date, posts, salary and Apply sat below the full eligibility text on a phone.                                                                                                                     | A key-facts line (deadline, posts, salary) and actions (Apply, Save, Add to calendar, Share) under the title.                                                                                                                       |
| Medium   | No way to get reminded outside the site.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | "Add to calendar" downloads an `.ics` event ending at the deadline, with reminders 1 day and 2 hours before (unit-tested).                                                                                                          |
| Medium   | Sharing a circular (common through WhatsApp) meant copying the URL by hand.                                                                                                                                                                                              | "Share" opens the phone's share sheet, or copies the link elsewhere.                                                                                                                                                                |
| Medium   | Circular filters took four stacked rows, about a full phone screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Search and button on one row, category and deadline side by side: two rows.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Medium   | **One tap started the timed exam,** with no reminder of the rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A "Ready to start?" dialog shows questions, minutes, negative marking (and whether to skip), that the timer keeps running, and that answers autosave.                                                                               |
| Medium   | The Exams page put a large "practice" banner above today's exam, and "All exams" repeated today's exam.                                                                                                                                                                  | Today comes first. The list is now "Previous days" (the API returns exams before today).                                                                                                                                            |
| Low      | Parent topics (e.g. Digital Logic) showed an empty "Notes" heading.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | A pointer to the subtopics instead.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Data     | Test circulars ("E2E Test Ministry") appeared in the running list.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | These were local browser-test leftovers, not code; removed. E2E tests already use an isolated schema.                                                                                                                               |

Checked and fine: registration and login (errors, return to the circular after "Log in to save"), the exam runner
(answer grid, autosave, submit summary), result review, learning progress (topics link to their notes), past papers.

## Still open (recommended next)

1. **Push and email deadline reminders.** Calendar files help, but reminders that arrive without the user acting are better (FCM for the mobile app).
2. **Age-eligibility check on circulars.** Government posts set age limits as of a date, and candidates calculate this by hand. A date-of-birth field plus an "eligible / over by N days" badge would answer "can I apply?" at a glance.
3. **Bangla UI for the web app.** The content is already bilingual.
4. **Order subjects by goal.** For example, technical subjects first for IT candidates; today only the dashboard uses the goal.
